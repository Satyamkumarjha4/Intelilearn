Questions

How does Socra know my level?

You answer a 5-question Quick check, or rate yourself with stars. Socra uses that to decide where your lesson starts and how deep it goes. Your Test score later tells it whether to suggest an easier or a more advanced lesson.

How do credits work?

Credits pay for what Socra makes for you. A full lesson with its Test is 10, a retake is 2, a sheet is 2 to 3 and a quick quiz is 1 to 4. Every button shows its cost before you click.

Is it free to start?

Yes. You get 30 credits when you sign up, enough for about 3 full lessons, with no card needed.

Do credits expire?

Your 30 sign-up credits never do. Credits you earn last 90 days, credits you buy last a year, and plan credits last for their plan's period. We remind you before any run out.

Will I be charged automatically?

Never. Every plan is prepaid and nothing renews on its own.

What if a lesson fails to build?

You get your credits back automatically.

Who is Socra for?

School and college students, and anyone early in their career learning something new.

Is my data private?

Your lessons are yours. Card and UPI details stay with the payment provider and never reach us. Read the Privacy Policy.
