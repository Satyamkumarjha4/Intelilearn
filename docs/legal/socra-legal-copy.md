## /privacy — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 25 October 2026**

Socra is an AI tutor that asks a few questions to find out what you already know, then builds a lesson for what you
don't. This policy explains what personal data Socra collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the
choices you have.

Socra is run by Satyam Jha, an individual based in India ("we", "us"). For the DPDP Act, we are the Data Fiduciary for
the personal data described here. Contact: satyamjha4@gmail.com.

### 1. Summary
- We collect only what we need to run your account and build your lessons.
- We never sell your data, never show ads, and never use your data to track you across other sites.
- Your name and email are never sent to AI providers.
- You can see, correct, download or delete your data by emailing us.

### 2. What we collect

**Account details.** Your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read
them.

**Learning profile.** What you tell us during onboarding or on your profile page: education level, class or year, field
of study, how you like to learn, your goal, lesson language, and any note you choose to add.

**Learning activity.** Topics you ask about, the notes you add to a lesson, your quick check answers, your test answers
(including written answers), your scores, the lessons and study sheets Socra makes for you, and their version history.

**Technical data.** Your IP address (used only to limit how often sign-in and lesson requests can be made, to protect
the service), basic server logs, and error reports when a lesson fails to load.

**Cookies and browser storage.** We use only what is needed to run Socra:

| Name | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `lc_access` | Keeps you signed in (secure, httpOnly) | 30 minutes, renewed automatically |
| `lc_refresh` | Renews your sign-in (secure, httpOnly) | 7 days, renewed while you use Socra |
| `lc_session`, `lc_onboarded` | Tell the app whether to show sign-in or onboarding | Until you sign out |
| `csrftoken` | Protects forms against cross-site attacks | Up to 1 year |
| Browser storage | Remembers small things on your device, such as your theme, which lesson pages you've read, or a quiz in progress | Until you clear it |

We do not use advertising, analytics or social-media tracking cookies.

### 3. Why we use your data
- **To provide Socra:** create your account, keep you signed in, build quick checks, lessons, tests and study sheets, and
  grade your answers. (Basis: your consent when you sign up, and to provide the service you asked for.)
- **To personalise lessons:** your learning profile and quick check results set the level, examples, language and
  starting point of each lesson.
- **To keep Socra safe and working:** rate limiting, preventing abuse, fixing errors, and checking that generated lessons
  run safely.
- **To reply to you** when you contact us.

We do not use your personal data for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Lesson routes
(for example "foundations first") only change what Socra teaches you next.

### 4. AI providers and other services
To create content, Socra sends **lesson requests** to AI model providers. A lesson request contains your topic, any
note you added, your learning profile (education level, class or year, field, learning preference, goal, language and
profile note) and, where needed, your quiz or test answers. **It never contains your name or email.**

Depending on how Socra is configured, these providers may include:

- Google (Gemini models)
- Groq
- OpenAI and Anthropic, if enabled

These providers process requests on our behalf and may do so outside India. We choose providers whose terms do not
allow them to use API data to train their models by default. We also check each provider's current terms.

To find images, Socra sends **search words only** (never personal data) to Wikimedia Commons, Openverse and, if enabled,
Giphy.

Socra's database and servers are hosted with a cloud provider. This may be Supabase or the hosting service in use at
the time.

If optional tracing tools are switched on to debug lesson quality (LangSmith or Langfuse), they receive the same lesson
request data as the AI providers, never your name or email.

We share personal data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

### 5. How long we keep it
- Account, profile, lessons and answers: for as long as you have an account.
- When you delete a lesson, its data is deleted. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data
  within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer.
- Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis within 30 days of deletion.
- Server logs and AI request logs: kept for up to 90 days. AI request logs store technical details (model, timing,
  tokens, errors), not your name or email.

### 6. Children and students
Socra is built for learners from Class 6 upwards, so some users are under 18. Under Indian law, anyone under 18 is a
child, and their personal data may be processed only with the **verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian**.

- If you are under 18, please use Socra only with your parent's or guardian's permission. A parent or guardian should
  create the account or confirm consent when we ask.
- We collect only what is needed for learning.
- We do not track children's behaviour across the web, do not profile children for advertising, and do not show ads.
- If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child is using Socra without your consent, email us. We will delete
  the account.

### 7. Your rights
Under the DPDP Act (and the GDPR, if you are in the EU or UK) you can:

- ask what personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with;
- correct or update it (most of it you can edit on your profile page);
- delete your account and data;
- withdraw consent at any time (this means closing your account);
- nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act yourself;
- get a copy of your data in a common format;
- raise a complaint with us and, if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India (or your local data
  protection authority in the EU or UK).

Email satyamjha4@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will reply within 30 days. To protect you, we may ask
you to confirm the request.

### 8. Security
- Passwords are hashed.
- Sign-in cookies are httpOnly and protected against cross-site requests.
- All traffic uses HTTPS.
- Lesson code made by AI is security-scanned, tested in an isolated browser and run in a sandbox that cannot reach the
  internet or your account.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board
of India without delay, as the law requires.

### 9. Grievance officer
Satyam Jha, satyamjha4@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.

### 10. Changes
If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will
also tell you in the app or by email before they apply.
