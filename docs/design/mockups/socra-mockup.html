The two stages of photosynthesis

generated lesson · sandboxed

Light meets leaf

Every leaf is a small solar factory. It takes in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil, and uses sunlight to turn them into glucose, the sugar a plant lives on. Oxygen is released as a by-product.

All of this happens inside tiny green structures called chloroplasts. The green colour comes from chlorophyll, the pigment that catches the light.

The equation

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Two stages, one goal: making sugar

Photosynthesis happens in two linked stages. First, the light-dependent reactions on the thylakoid membranes capture light energy and store it in ATP and NADPH. Then the Calvin cycle in the stroma spends that energy to build glucose from CO₂.

Key idea

Light stage = charge the batteries. Calvin cycle = spend the charge to build sugar.

Try it: light intensity vs. rateInteractive graph

Limiting factors

A plant can only go as fast as its scarcest ingredient allows. Turn up the light and the rate rises, until something else runs short. That shortage is called the limiting factor.

The three usual suspects are light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature. Greenhouse growers raise all three to push crops to grow faster.

Key idea

When the graph flattens, adding more of that factor won't help. Find what's running short.

Quadratic equations

Class 10 · Maths

The quadratic formula

x = −b ± √(b2 − 4ac)2a
D > 0Two different real roots
D = 0Two equal real roots
D < 0No real roots

Key terms

Standard form
ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0
Discriminant (D)
b² − 4ac. Tells you how many real roots there are.
Sum and product of roots
α + β = −b/a and αβ = c/a

Common mistakes

  • Dropping the minus sign in −b when b is already negative.
  • Dividing only part of the top line by 2a.
  • Forgetting to move every term to one side before using the formula.

Fastest method

Factorise first
If you can spot two numbers that multiply to ac and add to b.
Use the formula
When factorising isn't obvious within 30 seconds.

Remember

Check the discriminant first. It tells you what kind of answer to expect before you solve.
